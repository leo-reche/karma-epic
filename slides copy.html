From idea to sketch

The Karma-LLMifier

  1. 1

    The problem

    Talks and interviews hold useful information, but you must watch the whole video to find it.

  2. 2

    The insight

    Most AI tools work better with text than with video.

  3. 3

    The idea

    A simple website that turns a video into a text file you can ask questions about.

  4. 4

    The design

    One page: upload on the left, download on the right. The button is grey until the file is ready.

Reveal the four points one at a time. Click the sketch to enlarge it, and click again to close it.

Key talking points

From video to answers

1

Upload a video

Add your video, usually an MP4, and wait while the app processes it.

2

Download one file

You get a Markdown (.md) file as soon as the Download button turns on.

3

Everything in it

The full transcript, times to find each part in the video, and a few screenshots of important moments.

4

Ask your favourite AI

Give it the file and ask questions about the video, without watching it all.

Design decisions

Why we built it this way

1

Not a chatbot

Everyone already has an AI. We give it what it needs instead of building another one.

2

Video becomes knowledge

The idea is to transform what is in a video into knowledge you can use.

3

No Python needed

Users can run our Python script without knowing any Python.

We are not competing with the AI tools people already use. The value is turning video into knowledge, with no technical skills required.
